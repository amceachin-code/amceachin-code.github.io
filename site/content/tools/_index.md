Free tools for students, parents, and guardians. Each one runs in your browser, with nothing to sign up for.

## Making Sense of Grades and Test Scores {.tool-heading}

A guide for parents whose child's report card grades and test scores tell different stories. Pick the pattern that matches your student to see what it might mean, questions to ask the school, and what to look for next.\
**Open the tool:** [Making Sense of Grades and Test Scores](/grade-inflation/)

## Math Worksheets {.tool-heading}

Printable fact practice for multiplication and division. Each sheet is shuffled so every fact appears about equally often.

### Multiplication {.tool-heading}

Pick the times tables and the number of problems, then print.\
**Open the tool:** [Times Table Worksheets](/multiplication-worksheet/)

### Division {.tool-heading}

Pick the numbers to divide by and the range of answers, then print. Every answer is a whole number.\
**Open the tool:** [Division Worksheets](/multiplication-worksheet/division/)

**Want your own copy?** The code is free on [GitHub](https://github.com/amceachin-code/multiplication-worksheet). You can download it to use offline, see how it works, or suggest a change.

## Times Tables Games {.tool-heading}

Quick, friendly times tables practice for grades 2 to 5, on a phone, a tablet, or a school Chromebook. Learn a table step by step, practice twenty questions, take a speed test, earn a printable diploma, or walk Leo the dog down a street to the park, where every right answer gets him past something. Missed facts come back later, and the chart shows what is known. No ads, nothing to sign up for, and progress stays on the device.\
**Open the tool:** [Times Tables Games](/times-tables-games/)
